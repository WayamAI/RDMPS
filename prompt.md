## 💡 Quick Tips for Generating with Midjourney / Stable Diffusion / Google Imagen
- **Aspect Ratios:** Use `--ar 1:1` for square diagram blocks and `--ar 16:9` for section hero backgrounds.
- **Color Consistency:** Always retain `#EA580C` (Safety Orange), `#141414` (Deep Slate/Graphite), and `#2563EB` (Full System Blue).
- **Backgrounds:** Use `"isolated on clean dark graphite background"` or `"isolated on clean white studio background"` to easily composite into web apps.
